04 — FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

Does my APK leave my device?

No. The product is designed for client-side processing: the APK is processed in your browser rather than uploaded to an analysis server.

Is there a recommended APK size?

There is no hard limit stated by the product, but APKs under 500 MB are recommended for better performance. Larger packages depend more heavily on the memory and processing power of your device.

Does it fully decompile an APK into Java source?

No. The analyzer focuses on extraction and analysis of package contents, including the manifest, DEX files and resources. Full Java-source decompilation requires dedicated tools such as JADX or APKTool.

Is it free?

Yes. APK Analyzer Pro is presented as free and open source, with no subscriptions or premium features.

Which browsers work?

Modern Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge and Opera are supported. Mobile browsers are supported as well.
